Cartoon Siege

Build an army, hold your fortress, break theirs.

Free · No download · No account

A free side-scrolling siege game you play in your browser, on a computer or a phone. Send out troops, fire the crossbow, call down powers and storm the enemy fortress across six worlds.

What awaits you

30 battles, 6 worlds

From green meadows to the volcano and the frozen peaks, each world ends with its own guardian.

25 troops to upgrade

Spearmen, wizards, robots, dragons… Every troop changes shape as it levels up, and five are legendary.

Play the villains

Switch sides and lead the monsters in their own campaign, with their own cards and powers.

Duel a friend

Share a room code and fight live: one leads the heroes, the other the villains.

How to play

  1. Earn mana over time and spend it on troops at the bottom bar.
  2. Drag the aim and fire your crossbow to stop what gets through.
  3. Use powers when they charge, then bring down the enemy fortress.
  4. Win gold, upgrade your army and the next world opens.
